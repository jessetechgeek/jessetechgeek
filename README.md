## 🚀 Selected products

### 📱 [SubTrack](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ashersoft.subtrack) · subscription tracker on Google Play
The first consumer product shipped on the B2C SaaS Foundation. It tracks recurring subscriptions and renewal costs for end users, validates the Foundation's RevenueCat-based purchase flow end to end, and has 1,000+ backend tests of its own.

### 📦 B2C SaaS Foundation · NuGet platform
A packaged, versioned .NET platform (currently 0.3.1) covering auth, entitlements/billing and background jobs. It drives three client surfaces (Next.js, React admin, Flutter).
- JWT and cookie auth with refresh tokens, OTP password reset, MFA (TOTP and recovery codes), social login, account linking and self-service account deletion/data export.
- Multi-provider billing (Paddle and Dodo) with usage-based entitlement limits, tiered pricing, RevenueCat add-ons and idempotent webhooks.
- A metered AI service (Python plus .NET-side usage gating) and a Starter.CLI that scaffolds a new vertical-slice feature (40+ files) in one command.
- **4,607 backend tests** across 8 xUnit projects, plus client suites for React admin (263), Flutter (358) and Next.js (173).

### 🛒 [Shopilent](https://github.com/jessetechgeek/shopilent) · open-source e-commerce platform (archived)
Clean Architecture and DDD with CQRS (EF Core writes, Dapper reads), Redis cache-aside, a PostgreSQL read/write split and the transactional outbox pattern. Load-tested at **458 req/s (p95 311ms)** under 100 concurrent users with **0% failures** across 34,990 requests, on a single $4 Hetzner ARM VPS. It has **4,000+ automated tests**.

### 📰 UBE News App · client project on Google Play
An AI news aggregator. Flutter/GetX client with a Node.js/Express backend, a pipeline that runs every 6 hours to fetch and AI-neutralize articles with OpenAI, and on-demand translation and text-to-speech in 5+ languages via Google Cloud APIs.
